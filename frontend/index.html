FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our admission, fees, and learning modes.

How do I apply for tuition admission?

Simply click the "Apply for Admission" button, fill in your student and academic preferences in the multi-step form, and submit. You will receive a unique Application ID to track your admission status in real-time.

Can I choose between online and offline classes?

Yes! We offer 100% Online, In-Center Offline, and Hybrid options. Students who enroll in Hybrid can attend in person or switch to live Google Meet sessions whenever needed.

What is the average batch size?

We maintain strict limits of 15 to 25 students per batch depending on the grade and subject, ensuring the teacher can engage directly with every student.

Are study materials and test series included in the fee?

Yes, all enrolled students receive digital study notes, formula sheets, weekly homework worksheets, and full access to our periodic mock test series at no extra charge.
